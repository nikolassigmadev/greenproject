# H2 — Trase commercial-use permission

**To:** info@trase.earth
**Subject:** Permission request — Trase trade-flow data in a consumer app

---

Hello,

I maintain GoodScan, a consumer app that shows shoppers what is publicly known
about where the ingredients in a food product come from, together with an
explicit indication of how strong the evidence is for each claim.

I would like to request permission to use Trase trade-flow data, and I
understand from your open data terms that commercial use requires written
permission.

**What I would use it for.** Roughly 3–5% of food products declare an ingredient
origin, so for most products the honest answer today is "not disclosed". Trase
trade-flow statistics would let the app say something materially more useful for
those products — for example, that a given share of a country's cocoa imports
originate in a particular producing country.

**How it would be presented.** This is the part I would want you to hold me to.
Trade-flow data would only ever produce a clearly-marked *inferred* claim, drawn
differently from a declared one, and captioned as a statement about commodity
flows rather than about a company. Concretely, the wording would be of the form
"Indonesia imports X% of its cocoa from Côte d'Ivoire (Trase, <year>)" — never
"this product is sourced from Côte d'Ivoire". Trase would be cited on every
claim derived from it.

**What I am asking:**

1. Whether commercial use is permitted for this purpose, and on what terms.
2. Any attribution wording or citation format you require.
3. Whether a precomputed derived table — commodity × importing country ×
   exporting country shares, bundled with the app so it works offline —
   is within scope, or whether you would prefer live API use.
4. Any restrictions on how the inference may be phrased that you would like
   applied.

GoodScan is currently free to use. I am asking now rather than later so the
answer can shape the design instead of forcing a retraction.

Until I hear back, Trase data is not used in the app.

Thank you for your time.

Best regards,
Nikolas Dzhovanis
GoodScan
