# H1 — Universal Mill List licence terms

**To:** palmoil_traceability@ra.org
**Subject:** Licence terms for the Universal Mill List (palm oil)

---

Hello,

I maintain GoodScan, a consumer app that shows shoppers what is publicly known
about where the ingredients in a food product come from, and how strong the
evidence for each claim is.

I would like to use the Universal Mill List to place palm oil mills on a map
within the app, attributed to Rainforest Alliance. Before doing so I need to
confirm the licence, and I could not find terms stated on the distribution page
or in the accompanying documentation.

Specifically, I would like written confirmation of:

1. Whether the UML may be redistributed as part of an application, and under
   what licence or terms.
2. Whether commercial use is permitted. GoodScan is currently free; I am asking
   so I do not have to unwind the integration later if that changes.
3. The attribution wording you would like used wherever mill data appears.
4. Whether derived datasets — for example a barcode-to-mill index built from the
   UML joined to other sources — carry any share-alike or notification
   obligation.

For context on how it would be used: the app never presents an inferred origin
as a declared one. Every claim is rendered with its evidence tier, mill
locations would be shown as mill locations rather than as ingredient origins,
and anything we cannot substantiate is shown explicitly as undisclosed.

I am happy to work within whatever terms you specify, including not using the
data at all. Until I hear back the UML is not included in the app.

Thank you for your time.

Best regards,
Nikolas Dzhovanis
GoodScan
